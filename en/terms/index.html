What LifeCommit is

LifeCommit helps you keep habits, to-dos, goals and your calendar — on your own, with friends and in groups. It runs as a Mini App in Telegram and the bot @LifeCommit_bot; apps for iPhone and Android are on the way. It’s made by the LifeCommit team — «we» below.

Who can use it

You need a Telegram account: you sign in to LifeCommit with it. You can use the service if you’re allowed to use Telegram in your country.

Price and limits

LifeCommit is free for now. Voice and free-text parsing is limited to 20 times a day (one limit for the app and the bot), and a voice message can be up to 90 seconds. If paid features appear, we’ll tell you in advance what they include; you won’t pay for anything you didn’t choose yourself.

Your data is yours

Everything you create — habits, to-dos, goals, check-ins — belongs to you. You let us store and process it only as far as the service needs: to show it to you, sync it with calendars, parse voice, and show what you opened to groups and friends.

Groups and friends

  • Group members see the group’s shared to-dos, who they’re assigned to, who checked them and contributions to a shared goal, plus members’ Telegram names and photos.
  • If you connect the bot to a Telegram chat, it will post the group’s to-dos for the day and an evening summary there. The group’s owner and admins can connect and disconnect the chat.
  • Friends see your overall map of days without titles and the habits you allowed them to see.
  • You’re responsible for what you post in groups. Only invite people who want to join; a friend request can be declined and a person can be blocked.

  • trying to access other people’s data, breaking or overloading the service, or scraping data automatically;
  • pretending to be someone else.
