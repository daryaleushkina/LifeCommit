Together

Shared to-dos with your team, family and friends

Who prepares the report, whose turn it is today, how much you have saved for the vacation. Assign a to-do to someone, take turns, or give everyone their own.

The bot works in your group’s Telegram chat too: the day’s to-dos in the morning, check-offs with a button right in the chat, and new to-dos by voice in reply to the bot.
