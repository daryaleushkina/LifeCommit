Privacy policy

Updated October 4, 2026

LifeCommit is an app for habits, to-dos, goals and your calendar. It runs in Telegram (a Mini App and the bot @LifeCommit_bot). This page explains what data we keep, why, where it goes and how to delete it. The service is run by the LifeCommit team — «we» below.

In short

  • We keep only what the app needs to work: your Telegram profile and what you create in the app yourself.
  • No ads, no tracking counters or trackers, and we don’t sell or share data for advertising.
  • Voice and text you want parsed go to recognition services (Cloudflare Workers AI and Google Gemini). We don’t keep voice recordings.
  • You can delete your account and all your data with the «Delete account» button in your profile.

What we keep

Your Telegram profile

When you open LifeCommit, Telegram gives us your numeric id, first name, last name (if set), username, profile photo link and language. We also take your phone’s time zone so your day starts and reminders arrive on your time. Telegram doesn’t give us your phone number, and we don’t ask for it.

What you create

  • habits and goals: titles, schedule, daily target, check-ins by day and their history;
  • to-dos: title, day, time, repeat, done mark;
  • groups: name, members and their roles, shared to-dos, who checked what, contributions to a shared goal, and if you connected the bot to a chat — that chat’s id and title;
  • friends: requests, your friends list, blocked people, which habits you show to friends;
  • settings: reminder times, when your day starts.

Apple and Google calendars — only if you connect them

  • Apple (iCloud): your Apple ID email and the app-specific password you created for LifeCommit. The password is stored only encrypted (AES-GCM); only our server has the key.
  • Google: an access key (refresh token), also stored only encrypted.
  • The list of your calendars (names, colors) and their events: title, time, duration, repeat, place, call link, attendee names (up to 12) and the start of the description (up to 600 characters). Events show up for you as to-dos.

Service data

  • a daily count of voice parses, to keep the daily limit;
  • marks of which reminders were already sent;
  • technical server logs (requests and errors), kept by our host Cloudflare for a limited time.

On the lifecommit.app website itself we only remember your chosen theme (light or dark) in your browser. There are no tracking cookies.

Why

To show your habits, to-dos and calendar, count check-ins, send reminders (if you turned them on), run groups and friends, sync calendars and parse voice. The data isn’t used for anything else — not for ads and not for profiling.

Voice and free text

When you say or type a phrase in the app or to the bot:

  • a voice recording is turned into text by the Whisper model in Cloudflare Workers AI;
  • the text (and for groups — group titles and member names, to know whom a to-do is for) is sent to the Google Gemini API, and if it doesn’t answer — to the Llama model in Cloudflare Workers AI; they turn the phrase into a list of habits and to-dos.

We don’t keep the recording or the original text — only what you add from the suggested list stays. A voice message you send to the bot stays in your chat with the bot in Telegram, like any message.

The Gemini API is currently used on Google’s free tier. Under Google’s terms, data sent on the free tier may be used to improve Google’s products. If that matters to you, type instead of using voice.

Calendars: what goes to Apple and Google

LifeCommit writes to your calendar only your own to-dos that have a time, and changes or deletes them when you change or delete the to-do. Events that came from your calendar are changed there only if you changed or deleted them in LifeCommit yourself.

Google Calendar is connected through Google sign-in. LifeCommit asks for two permissions: to read and change events (calendar.events) and to see the list of calendars (calendar.calendarlist.readonly).

LifeCommit’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Calendar data is used only to show and sync your events and to-dos: it isn’t shared with third parties (other than the hosting the service runs on), isn’t used for ads, isn’t sold and isn’t used to train AI models. People don’t read it, except when you ask us for help, or when security or the law requires it.

When you disconnect a calendar in LifeCommit, we delete the stored password or key and the events that came from that calendar; for Google we also revoke the key. Your own to-dos stay.

Who sees your data

  • You. Everything in your account.
  • Group members. The group’s shared to-dos, who they’re assigned to, who checked them, contributions to a shared goal, your name and Telegram photo. Your personal habits and to-dos aren’t visible to a group unless you show a habit to it yourself.
  • A Telegram chat connected to a group. The bot posts the group’s to-dos for today there — with names when a to-do is assigned to someone or someone did it — and an evening summary without names.
  • Friends. Your overall map of days without titles; titles and check-ins only for habits you allowed them to see. To-dos and calendar events are never shown to friends.
  • Us. Only the server has access to the database; we look at data manually only when you ask for help or to fix a bug.

How long we keep it and how to delete it

We keep your data while you have an account. The «Delete account» button in your profile deletes your profile and all your data: habits, to-dos, check-ins, calendar connections together with passwords and keys, friends and requests, counters. Groups you owned pass to the next member, or are archived if there are no members left. Shared to-dos you created in a group stay in the group without an author; your check-ins and contributions in groups are deleted. To-dos already written to your Apple or Google calendar stay there — it’s your calendar.

You can delete a single habit or to-do in the app at any time. Deleting your chat with the bot in Telegram doesn’t delete your LifeCommit account — use the button in your profile for that.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data right in the app, delete it with «Delete account», and turn off calendars and reminders. To get a copy of your data or ask about it, write to us.

Children

LifeCommit isn’t meant for children younger than the age at which Telegram may be used in your country.

Changes

If we change this policy, the date at the top of the page changes. We’ll tell you about notable changes in the bot.
